How the value is worked out

Implied FDV = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity. The category multiple is the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of launched protocols in the same category, shrunk toward the global prior 0.246 with k = 3 and clamped to 0.02–3.0, so a thin category borrows strength instead of trusting one or two names. Growth is 1 + the 7-day TVL change, clamped to 0.5–2.0 (1.0 when unreported). Maturity is 1 + 0.10 per year since the protocol was first listed, capped at 1.3 (1.0 when unlisted). Nothing here is a prediction that a token will launch, or when: it is what a launch would be worth if the protocol’s record stayed as it is.

Comparables exclude bridges, liquid staking and restaking, CEXs, versioned names, whole-word wrapped / staked / staking names, and exchange-prefixed names — none of those is a clean launch peer. The board itself holds only protocols that record no token in the snapshot; that is the record, never proof a protocol has no token. Every watchlist protocol is on the board, plus early finds below the $50M floor that pass the same screen.
